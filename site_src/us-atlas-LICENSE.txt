Connecticut planning regions (09110-09190) in counties-albers-10m.json are
taken from growella/us-counties-10m-topojson
(https://github.com/growella/us-counties-10m-topojson), which carries the
official State of Connecticut planning-region boundaries, projected here to
the same Albers USA layout (scale 1300, translate 487.5, 305) and simplified
to the detail of the surrounding counties. They replace the eight retired
Connecticut counties (09001-09015).
